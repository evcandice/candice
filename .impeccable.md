# Impeccable — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
People deciding whether to trust or build a private family AI. They land mostly on a
phone, often in the evening, skimming. Secondary: technical folks curious how it's built.
The job: understand in ~20 seconds what Candice is, believe it's real (not vaporware),
and feel it's safe to hand a household to.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **warm, private, quietly-sharp.**
- Warm: it's a member of a family, not a product. Texts back like a person who knows you.
- Private: runs at home, masks personal data before any cloud call, your life stays yours.
- Quietly-sharp: five-steps-ahead competence without showing off or shouting specs.

Voice: plain, warm, lowercase-ish, zero corporate. Never uses a pronoun for Candice —
always "Candice", never she/he/it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm editorial, "dim reading room" — a dark theme derived from the evening-phone context,
executed as warm charcoal + paper-warm ink + a single brass accent, NOT the default
dark-mode-with-neon-glow AI reflex. No glows, no gradients-for-decoration, no purple/cyan.
Reference feel: warm print editorial (danielmiessler's paper-toned warmth, translated to a
warm dark). Anti-reference: generic AI SaaS landing (eyebrow kicker, 01/02/03 labels,
icon-tiles, Geist, gradient text, glassmorphism).
Keep the hand-painted octopus doodle — it's a real raster asset and the brand's soul.

### Design Principles
1. Earn hierarchy with type and space, not scaffolding (no eyebrows, no numbered labels).
2. Every element is real or it's cut — no decorative grids, glows, or fake status pulses.
3. Commit each surface to one treatment: a defined edge OR a soft elevation, never both.
4. The proof is the product: a real, unedited Candice conversation is the centerpiece.
5. Readable first: WCAG AA contrast, ~65–75ch measure, functional text ≥13px.

## Decisions (flip any of these — they're tokens)
- Theme: warm dark. (Light/paper is a viable alt; one token flip if wanted.)
- Display font: Young Serif. Body font: Hanken Grotesk. No monospace.
- Accent: brass. Blush retained only as a tiny secondary tint.
- Layout: bento — video anchor + three beats in one block; no full-height half-empty rows.
