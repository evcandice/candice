How privacy works See it work

Your family's privacy matters.

Memories are stored and searched on the Mac. Before a question goes to Claude, Apple's on-device model reads it on the Mac and hides the personal details it finds: names, phone numbers, emails, ID numbers, bank and card numbers, addresses, number plates, dates of birth and organisations.

Our latest test shows 98.65% of the personal details were hidden.1

What the model sees

"did ⟦PERSON_1⟧ reply from ⟦EMAIL_1⟧ about the plumbing quote?"

What you see

"yep, Sam emailed back from sam@… this morning. the quote came in under budget."

A simple question, worked out properly.

Gigi asks how much paint a bedroom needs. The family and the question are made up. The answers are Candice's own. The notes beside the chat show what Candice remembered, how the sums were done, and where Candice stopped to ask.

Candice remembers

Months ago, Gigi mentioned that the hallway paint covers 12 m² per litre and comes in 2.5 litre tins. Here Gigi only says "same paint as last time", and Candice fills in the rest.

Candice shows the working out

Asked how many tins to buy, Candice did the sums step by step and said where the answer was tight:

  • Walls 31.2 m². 13 m around the room, 2.4 m high.
  • Gaps About 28 m² left. Roughly 3 m² off for the door and window.
  • Coats About 4.7 litres. Two coats is about 56 m², at 12 m² per litre.
  • Tins 2 tins, just. 5 litres covers 60 m², with only 0.3 litres spare.
  • 3 tins once Gigi wants a margin: 7.5 litres, enough for about 90 m².

Candice asks before doing more

Candice says plainly that buying the paint is up to Gigi, then offers a reminder before Saturday and waits for an answer. Anything that reaches outside the house gets the same question first.

Candice asks first.

Anything that cannot be undone, like a deletion or unlocking a door, waits for a yes, and Candice spells out exactly what will happen first. Any adult in the house can text "Candice, stop" and everything pauses. Only the person who looks after Candice can start things again. Candice reads emails and web pages as information only. If one leads to an action, a person has to say yes first.
