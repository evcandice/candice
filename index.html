It does the work

Asked how many light panels each room needs, Candice sized it from the real spec and standard lux targets, not a guess:

  • Living2 panels. 25–30m² at 300 lux needs ~8,000–9,000 lumens.
  • Bedrooms1 each. 200 lux target, one panel covers 3,200 lumens.
  • Kitchen1 + task. 400 lux for counters, add under-cabinet light.
  • SkipBathrooms (not wet-rated) and the foyer (overkill).
  • 6 panels, RM2,154 total, and a flag that the smart version's Zigbee may not pair with your Aqara before you buy.

You own it

It runs from your home. The budget, the messages, the plans stay yours. No cloud company reading over your shoulder, no account to become the product of. A member of the household, not a monthly fee.

Your name never leaves in the clear.

When a request needs a cloud model, the private details don't travel with it. They're masked on the way out and restored on the way back, so the model reasons without ever seeing who it's about.
