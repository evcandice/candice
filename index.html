01 — Candice knows you

Not a chatbot that forgets.

Candice holds the running context of your life — the renovation budget and how it's split, that the new place is still waiting on its re-inspection, the balcony you measured last week. So when you ask, Candice is already standing on everything that came before.

"You don't repeat yourself to Candice. Candice was there the first time."
02 — Candice does the work

Five steps ahead, not one answer behind.

Give Candice a messy task with real constraints, and back comes something you can act on — then the thing you hadn't gotten to yet. No shopping list handed over. Candice reasons through it room by room, checks it against the budget, and flags the re-inspection nobody's booked — because that's what blocks the whole timeline.

How Candice figured it out

  • Started from the floor plan and how each room gets used — not a device catalog.
  • Chose Matter / Thread so it runs off a HomePod border router — no server, no monthly anything.
  • Weighed every device against the furnishing budget, not the renovation-works budget.
  • Cross-referenced the developer's handover fittings so nothing gets bought twice.
  • Used the measurements already on file instead of asking again.
03 — You own Candice

Runs from your home, on your terms.

Your family's life — the budget, the messages, the plans — stays yours. No cloud company reading over your shoulder, no account to be the product of. Candice is a member of the household, not a subscription. And before anything that can't be undone, Candice checks with you first.

"A member of the family. Not a monthly fee."
04 — Your privacy, by design

Names and numbers never leave in the clear.

When a request needs a cloud model, your private details don't travel with it. They're masked on the way out and restored on the way back — so the model does the reasoning without ever seeing who, or what, it's really about.
